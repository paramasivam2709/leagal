Who We Are

A Legacy of Trusted Legal Counsel

Founded with a simple conviction — that great legal advice should be clear, practical and personal — Stackly Legal Consulting has grown into a multi-practice consultancy serving individuals, startups and established enterprises.

From our chambers in Chennai, our attorneys combine courtroom experience with modern consulting methods, so every client gets both strategic vision and meticulous execution.

  • 15+ years of combined practice
  • Multi-disciplinary legal team
  • Strict ethical standards
  • Clients across India & abroad
